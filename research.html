Research

Our research focuses on food safety, food quality, meat science, and emerging analytical technologies.

Chemical Food Safety

Our research investigates chemical hazards in foods and develops approaches to improve food safety and quality.

Meat Safety and Quality

We study technologies and approaches to improve the safety, quality, and shelf life of fresh and processed meats.

Consumer Sensory Science

Our research explores consumer perception, sensory properties, and factors influencing food acceptance.

Emerging Analytical Technologies

We investigate advanced analytical technologies, including mass spectrometry and MALDI-TOF MS, for food safety and microbiological applications.
